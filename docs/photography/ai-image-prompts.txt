GLOBAL GATE STUDENTS NETWORK — AI IMAGE GENERATION PROMPTS
============================================================
Purpose: Replace generic/mismatched stock photography (current site uses a Saudi
graduation photo, a Southeast Asian campus photo, etc.) with imagery that actually
reflects the audience (Sierra Leonean / West African applicants) while keeping the
"students in Hungary" setting and the site's warm, editorial, burgundy-and-gold vibe.

WHERE FREE STOCK PHOTOS FALL SHORT (checked before writing these prompts)
------------------------------------------------------------------------
- Unsplash, Pexels, Pixabay, and Freepik all have large free libraries for
  "African student" (generic, no Hungary/Europe context) and separately for
  "Budapest" / "Hungary university" (generic, no African subjects). Searched
  both directly.
- Nobody has photographed the specific combination this brand needs — a West
  African / Sierra Leonean student on a Hungarian campus or against Budapest
  architecture. That combination essentially does not exist as free stock.
- Recommendation: use free stock ONLY for neutral texture/detail shots (a
  passport on a desk, a boarding pass, autumn leaves on a Budapest street,
  the Danube, Parliament building exteriors) — never for a "hero" shot that
  needs a real represented student, since a viewer's eye goes straight to the
  face. For anything with a person meant to represent "our student," use the
  AI prompts below, OR real photos (see note at the bottom — always better
  when available).
- Good free-stock hunting grounds for the neutral/backdrop shots:
  https://unsplash.com/s/photos/budapest
  https://www.pexels.com/search/budapest/
  https://pixabay.com/images/search/budapest/
  https://unsplash.com/s/photos/african-student (for general mood-boarding only,
  not for final site use as a "this is our student" image)

HOW TO USE THESE PROMPTS
-------------------------
- Recommended tools: Adobe Firefly (image_generate tool already available in
  this environment) or Midjourney v6/7 for photorealism; Ideogram or GPT
  Image for anything needing legible text-free composition. Firefly is the
  safest for commercial use (trained on licensed/Adobe Stock content).
- Always generate at minimum 1600px on the long edge (site uses large hero
  crops). Ask for a 3:2 or 4:5 ratio depending on placement (noted per prompt).
- After generating, run a color grade pass (warm shadows, slightly desaturated,
  gentle burgundy/gold cast) so images feel like one consistent shoot, not
  stock-photo-of-the-week. Brand palette: burgundy #1f1210, gold #c9a24a,
  gold-soft #e4c57a, blush #f3e4e2, warm off-white background #fbfaf8.
- Global negative prompt to append everywhere: "no text, no watermark, no logo,
  no visible brand names, no exaggerated or stereotyped features, no uncanny
  hands, no extra fingers, not glossy/plastic AI look, avoid generic stock-photo
  poses, natural asymmetric candid framing."
- Always disclose AI-generated imagery is in use if asked by a platform (Meta/
  Google ad policies increasingly require this) — flagged again in the main plan.
